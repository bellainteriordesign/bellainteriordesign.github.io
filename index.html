About Bella Interior Design

Designing Complete Luxury Environments

Architecture, interiors, fit-out and furnishing coordinated through one carefully developed design process.

Established in 2022 and led by CEO, Architect and Chief Designer Shams, Bella Interior Design develops sophisticated environments for clients seeking highly personalized design and project delivery in Abu Dhabi.

A project may begin with an empty plot, an unfinished shell, a newly completed property or an existing interior requiring transformation. The design process can extend from concept development and master planning through architectural design, technical coordination, interior fit-out, bespoke furniture, furnishing and final handover.

Every interior is planned around the lifestyle of the client. Comfort, proportion, circulation, privacy, functionality, lighting, furniture placement and architectural relationships are considered alongside materials and decorative details.

This allows Bella Interior Design to create spaces that are not simply visually impressive but also practical, balanced and appropriate for everyday living.

Our Design Philosophy

Contemporary Luxury with Classical Character

Bella Interior Design combines contemporary sophistication with carefully selected classical and royal influences. The objective is not to reproduce one fixed design style, but to develop an individual identity for every property.

Symmetry, scale, proportion, layered lighting and architectural detailing are balanced with the functional needs of the client.

Refined gold accents, crystal lighting, natural stone, handcrafted furniture and tailored fabrics can create richness without allowing decorative elements to overwhelm the architecture.
